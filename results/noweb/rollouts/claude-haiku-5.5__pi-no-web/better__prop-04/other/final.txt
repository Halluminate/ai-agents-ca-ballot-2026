**Arguments for yes:** Supporters, such as the League of Women Voters of California and Consumer Watchdog, generally argue that public funding can reduce the influence of big donors and make it easier for candidates without wealthy backers to run.

**Arguments for no:** Opponents, such as the California Taxpayers Association and Howard Jarvis Taxpayers Association, generally argue that taxpayers shouldn't pay for political campaigns and that public money is better spent on other priorities.
