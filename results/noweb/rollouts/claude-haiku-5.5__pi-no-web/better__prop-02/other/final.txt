- Bigger reserves can strengthen the state's fiscal position and credit standing.

**Case for no:**
- Money in reserves isn't available for current spending or tax relief.
- A bigger cushion could make it easier to delay tough budget decisions or let spending grow in good years.
- Some may argue the reserve created in 2014 is already adequate.
