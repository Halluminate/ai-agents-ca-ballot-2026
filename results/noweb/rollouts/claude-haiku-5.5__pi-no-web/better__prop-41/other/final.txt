I won't pick yes or no, since the choice depends on how you weigh the tradeoffs, and reasonable people disagree.

**A yes vote** would prohibit new state taxes that exclude their revenue from the state spending limit (the Gann limit) and require pre-election and recurring audits of programs funded by new special taxes. **A no vote** would leave those rules as they are.

The core tradeoff is tighter constraints and oversight on new taxes and spending versus more flexibility for lawmakers and voters to fund programs through new taxes. Supporters include CalCPAs, the CalAsian Chamber of Commerce, and CalTax. Opponents include Bernie Sanders and SEIU United Healthcare Workers West. The fiscal impact is listed as unknown because it depends on future decisions.

For the full arguments, read the Secretary of State's Voter Information Guide and the Legislative Analyst's Office analysis.
