I don't give yes-or-no voting recommendations on ballot measures, but I can help you weigh it.

Currently, a recall ballot also elects the replacement at the same time. This measure would instead fill a recall vacancy through a later special election or an appointment.

**Arguments for it:**
- A separate replacement election can avoid a crowded race where a plurality winner takes office.
- Splitting the recall and the replacement into separate decisions may give voters a clearer choice.

**Arguments against it:**
- The current system fills the seat without a second election, which can be faster and cheaper.
- An appointment gives voters less direct say, and a special election could leave the office vacant for a while.

The fiscal impact is uncertain, with possible costs or savings in the millions depending on the office. The official guide also lists no supporter or opponent arguments, so nonpartisan sources like Vote411.org may help.

Your decision depends on whether you value a separate, direct election more than speed and lower cost.
