I know you asked for just yes or no, but I won't pick a side on a ballot measure for you. It comes down to how you weigh saving money now against spending it now, and that's your call. Here's the core tradeoff:

- **Yes** raises the cap on the state's rainy day fund (from 10% to 20% of tax revenue, as I understand it), so more is saved in good years. Supporters argue California's revenue swings sharply, and a bigger cushion would protect schools, health care, and public safety from deep cuts during downturns.
- **No** keeps the current cap, so less is set aside and more stays available for spending now. Critics argue that locking away more money limits the state's ability to meet needs today and reduces budget flexibility.

For the full details and both sides' arguments, check the official California Voter Information Guide (voterguide.sos.ca.gov), which includes a nonpartisan analysis. If you tell me what matters most to you, I can help you work through which way that points.
