**What the Measure Does**
According to the provided ballot text, Proposition 42 is a constitutional amendment that would ban the state from creating new taxes on the ownership of personal property (which includes things people own other than real estate). It would also prohibit new state taxes that apply retroactively to a taxpayer's past activities, and it would nullify any conflicting taxes enacted after January 1, 2026. The fiscal impact analysis notes that passing this measure could result in state tax revenues not increasing as much in the future as they otherwise might.

**Support for Proposition 42 (A "Yes" Vote)**
As listed in the ballot summary, supporters of the measure include the California Professional Firefighters, the State Building & Construction Trades Council of California, and AMVETS Department of California. A "yes" vote aligns with these groups in favoring constitutional limits that prevent the state from introducing new personal property taxes or retroactive tax measures.

**Opposition to Proposition 42 (A "No" Vote)**
According to the ballot text, opponents of the measure include U.S. Senator Bernie Sanders and SEIU United Healthcare Workers West. A "no" vote aligns with these opponents in rejecting the proposed constitutional restrictions, thereby maintaining the state's ability to enact these types of taxes and avoiding the potential limits on future tax revenue growth noted in the fiscal impact statement.
