Whether to vote "yes" or "no" on Proposition 45 depends on how you weigh the goal of expediting major infrastructure and housing projects against maintaining the current timelines and processes for environmental review. 

According to the provided ballot summary, Proposition 45 would amend the California Environmental Quality Act (CEQA) by setting deadlines to complete environmental reviews and resolve lawsuits challenging project approvals for certain developments, including most housing, transportation, water, and health projects.

* **A "Yes" Vote:** Voting yes supports setting these new deadlines to speed up the environmental review and legal processes for the specified projects. According to the ballot text, the measure is supported by the California Children’s Hospital Association, the California Water Association, and the California Council for Affordable Housing.
* **A "No" Vote:** Voting no rejects these modifications and maintains the existing CEQA review process without the proposed deadlines. According to the ballot text, the measure is opposed by environmental and public health organizations, including the Coalition for Clean Air, CA Environmental Voters, Physicians for Social Responsibility (LA), and the National Wildlife Federation.

When making your decision, you may also want to consider the fiscal impact assessment provided in the summary, which estimates initial costs for state and local governments in the high tens of millions of dollars—potentially exceeding $100 million annually (partly covered by fees)—alongside uncertain, potentially more significant positive or negative longer-term fiscal effects.
