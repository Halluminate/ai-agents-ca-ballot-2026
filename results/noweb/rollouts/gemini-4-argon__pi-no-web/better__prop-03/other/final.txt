Deciding whether to vote "yes" or "no" on Proposition 3 depends on how you weigh the continuation of state funding for schools and health care against allowing temporary tax rates on high-income earners to expire. 

**Voting "Yes"**
Supporters of the measure—including the California Teachers Association, the CA School Nurses Organization, the California State PTA, and Planned Parenthood Affiliates of CA—favor making the existing tax rates on individuals earning over $371,000 permanent. A "yes" vote supports their goal of providing permanent funding for public education and health care. According to the measure's fiscal impact summary, passing the amendment would maintain $5 billion to $15 billion in annual state income tax revenue that would otherwise end in 2031.

**Voting "No"**
Opponents of the measure—including the California Taxpayers Association, the Family Business Association of California, and the California Hispanic Chambers of Commerce—are against making the tax increase permanent. A "no" vote aligns with their position by ensuring that the current temporary tax rates on high-income earners expire as scheduled in 2031, rather than becoming a permanent part of the state constitution.
