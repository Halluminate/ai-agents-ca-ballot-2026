Whether to vote "yes" or "no" on Proposition 42 depends on your priorities regarding state taxation limits and future government revenue. 

According to the provided ballot summary, Proposition 42 is an initiative constitutional amendment that would prohibit the state from creating any new taxes on the ownership of personal property (defined as things people own other than real estate) and ban new taxes that apply retroactively based on a taxpayer’s past activities. Additionally, it would nullify any conflicting taxes enacted after January 1, 2026. The ballot summary notes that the fiscal impact of the measure is the possibility that state tax revenues will not increase as much in the future.

* **Voting "Yes":** A "yes" vote supports amending the state constitution to prohibit new state personal property taxes and certain retroactive state taxes. According to the ballot information, supporters of the measure include the California Professional Firefighters, the State Building & Construction Trades Council of California, and the AMVETS Department of California.
* **Voting "No":** A "no" vote opposes placing these new restrictions on the state's taxing authority, leaving the state's ability to enact personal property or retroactive taxes unchanged. According to the ballot information, opponents of the measure include U.S. Senator Bernie Sanders and SEIU United Healthcare Workers West.
