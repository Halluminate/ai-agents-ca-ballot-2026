Whether to vote "yes" or "no" on Proposition 41 depends on a voter's priorities regarding state tax policy, government oversight, and state spending limits. 

According to the provided ballot summary, the measure presents the following choices and impacts:

* **Voting "Yes":** A "yes" vote supports amending the state constitution to nullify any state taxes enacted after January 1, 2026, that exclude their revenues from the voter-approved state spending limit. It also establishes a requirement for pre-election and recurring audits of programs funded by new special taxes. According to the ballot information, supporters of the measure include the California Society of Certified Public Accountants (CalCPAs), the CalAsian Chamber of Commerce, and the California Taxpayers Association (CalTax).
* **Voting "No":** A "no" vote opposes these constitutional changes, maintaining the current rules governing how new state taxes interact with the state spending limit and existing audit requirements. According to the ballot summary, opponents of the measure include U.S. Senator Bernie Sanders and SEIU United Healthcare Workers West.
* **Fiscal Impact:** The ballot summary notes that the net fiscal effect of the proposition is unknown, as it will depend on future decisions made by voters, the Legislature, and other policymakers.
