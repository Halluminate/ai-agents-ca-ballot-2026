Deciding whether to vote "yes" or "no" on Proposition 43 depends on your perspective regarding local tax thresholds and funding for public services. 

According to the provided ballot information, a **"yes" vote** supports amending the constitution to increase the threshold required to pass voter-proposed local special taxes from a simple majority (over 50%) to a two-thirds vote, beginning January 1, 2027. As noted in the measure's fiscal impact summary, this higher vote threshold creates the possibility that local government tax revenues will not increase as much in the future. Supporters of Proposition 43 include the California Taxpayers Association, the Family Business Association of California, and the California Hispanic Chambers of Commerce.

Conversely, a **"no" vote** opposes this constitutional amendment, keeping the current rule that allows voter-proposed local special taxes to pass with a simple majority vote to raise revenues for local government services. Opponents of the measure include the California Professional Firefighters, the California Federation of Teachers, and the Nurse Alliance of SEIU CA.
