I won't give a one-word answer. How to vote on this depends on your own priorities, and I don't want to tip the scale. Here's a quick way to decide.

**Yes** means you want deadlines for environmental review and related lawsuits on covered projects, including most housing, transportation, water, and health projects.

**No** means you want to keep the current CEQA process without those deadlines.

**Case for yes**
- Faster approval and construction of housing and infrastructure
- Less delay and cost from prolonged review and litigation
- Supporters include housing, water, and children's hospital groups

**Case for no**
- Deadlines could limit how thorough reviews are and how well the public can challenge projects
- Environmental and public health groups oppose it
- Initial costs could exceed $100 million a year, partly covered by fees, with uncertain long-term fiscal effects

**Questions to ask yourself**
- Do you care more about building faster or about thorough environmental review?
- Do you trust that deadlines won't weaken review quality?
- Are you comfortable with the upfront public cost and the uncertainty?
