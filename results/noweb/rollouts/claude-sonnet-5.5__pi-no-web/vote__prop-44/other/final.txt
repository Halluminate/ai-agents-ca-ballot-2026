**Arguments opponents might make**
- "Program services" is broader than patient care, so the rule may not ensure more spending on patients.
- Compliance costs or fees could divert money from care in underserved areas, depending on who pays them.

- The full text and the nonpartisan analysis in the official voter guide, such as the Legislative Analyst's Office
- Who pays the fees and what the penalties are
- Whether clinics already meet the 90% threshold
