I'm not going to pick a side on this one. It's a contested trade-off that depends on your own priorities. Here is what each side points to:

**Reasons to vote yes**
- Deadlines for environmental review and for resolving lawsuits could speed up housing, transportation, water, and health projects.
- Supporters argue CEQA litigation is sometimes used to delay or block needed projects.

**Reasons to vote no**
- Shorter timelines could mean less thorough review and less room for communities to challenge projects, with possible effects on air quality, wildlife, and public health.
- The initial cost to state and local governments is likely in the high tens of millions of dollars, possibly over $100 million annually, only partly covered by fees. The longer-term fiscal effects are uncertain.

The question for you is whether faster project approval or stronger environmental review and legal recourse matters more. The measure's full text and the nonpartisan Legislative Analyst's report are worth reading before you decide.
