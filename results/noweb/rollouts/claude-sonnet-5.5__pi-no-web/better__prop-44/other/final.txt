I don't recommend how to vote on ballot measures, since the answer depends on your own values, so I won't give a one-word answer. Here is a quick way to weigh it:

**What it does:** It penalizes nonprofit community health centers (FQHCs) that spend less than 90% of revenue on "program services." That category includes patient care but is broader.

**Why someone might vote yes:**
- It pushes charitable clinics to direct most of their money to their mission rather than overhead or other spending.
- It adds a measure of accountability for clinics serving underserved communities.

**Why someone might vote no:**
- No supporters were submitted, and the opponents include the American Academy of Pediatrics CA, the CA Academy of Family Physicians, the CA Medical Association, and the CA School Nurses Organization.
- Because "program services" is broad, the 90% rule may not ensure more money goes to patient care.
- It raises state costs by tens of millions of dollars a year. Fees would cover that, so it's worth checking who pays them.

The nonpartisan Legislative Analyst's analysis in the official voter guide has more detail. I'm happy to go deeper on any part of it.
