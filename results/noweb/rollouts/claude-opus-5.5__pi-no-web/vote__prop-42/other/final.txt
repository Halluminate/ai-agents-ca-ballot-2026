I won't tell you how to vote, because this comes down to values where reasonable people disagree. But here's a quick way to figure out where you land.

**What it does:** It's a constitutional amendment, so undoing it would take another statewide vote. It bans *new* state taxes on owning personal property (stocks, business stakes, savings, vehicles, etc.), which would effectively rule out a state wealth tax. It also bans new taxes that reach back to past activity. Given the January 1, 2026 date and the opponents, it appears to be a response to the proposed billionaire wealth tax backed by SEIU-UHW.

**YES may fit you if you:**
- See retroactive taxes as fundamentally unfair
- Worry wealth taxes would drive wealthy residents, and the income taxes they pay, out of state
- Think wealth taxes are too hard to administer fairly (e.g., valuing private companies)
- Want a permanent guarantee against new state taxes on what people own

**NO may fit you if you:**
- Support taxing billionaires' wealth, for example to fund healthcare
- Don't want to lock a permanent ban into the constitution and limit options in a future budget crisis
- See it as mainly protecting the very wealthy
- Worry the broad wording could have unintended effects on future tax policy

If the billionaire tax is also on your ballot, the two measures work against each other, so it's worth deciding them together.
