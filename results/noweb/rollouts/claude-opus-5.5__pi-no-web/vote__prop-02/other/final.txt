I can't give you a yes or no, since how you vote is your call. Here's a quick breakdown to help you decide.

**What it does:** It lets California save more money in good years so it can avoid cuts during recessions.

**Reasons people vote Yes:**
- California's tax revenue swings a lot with the economy and stock market. A bigger cushion could protect schools, health care, and public safety during downturns.
- Larger reserves can help the state's credit rating and lower borrowing costs.
- Both business (LA Chamber) and labor (firefighters) support it, and no opposition argument was submitted.

**Reasons someone might vote No:**
- Money held in reserve isn't available for current programs or tax relief.
- Requiring bigger savings gives lawmakers less flexibility in good years.

**Rule of thumb:** If saving more for hard times appeals to you, that points to Yes. If you'd rather that money go to current needs or back to taxpayers, that points to No.
