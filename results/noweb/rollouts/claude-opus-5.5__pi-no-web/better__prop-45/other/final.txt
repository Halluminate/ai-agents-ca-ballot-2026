I won't give a one-word verdict on this one, because it depends on how you weigh competing priorities, and reasonable voters disagree.

- **Lean yes** if you mainly want housing, transit, water, and health projects built faster, and you see environmental reviews and lawsuits as a frequent cause of years-long delays.
- **Lean no** if you're more concerned that hard deadlines will rush environmental review, limit communities' ability to challenge harmful projects in court, and cost tens of millions of dollars a year (possibly over $100 million), only partly covered by fees.

The core question is whether you think the current review process more often blocks needed projects or stops harmful ones.
