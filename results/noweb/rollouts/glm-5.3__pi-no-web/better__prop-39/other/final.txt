I can't tell you how to vote — that's a judgment call that depends on your values. But here's a quick summary to help you decide:

**Vote YES if** you prioritize election security and believe requiring ID is a reasonable safeguard against fraud, and you accept the cost (tens to low hundreds of millions annually).

**Vote NO if** you're concerned the requirements could create barriers for eligible voters (e.g., those without ID, or mail voters who forget the ID digits), or you think the fiscal cost outweighs the benefit given that documented in-person voter fraud is rare.

You could also look up nonpartisan analyses from sources like your state's official voter guide or Ballotpedia for more detail.
