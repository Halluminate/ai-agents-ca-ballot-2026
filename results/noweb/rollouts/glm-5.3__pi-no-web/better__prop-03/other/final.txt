I can't tell you how to vote—that's a decision that depends on your own values and priorities, and this measure is genuinely contested.

If it helps you decide, here are the core considerations:

**A yes vote** keeps a tax on high earners (over ~$371,000) that would otherwise expire in 2031, preserving $5–15 billion annually, mainly for schools and health programs.

**A no vote** lets the tax expire in 2031, reducing state revenue but ending what opponents view as a temporary tax that became permanent.

Key questions to ask yourself: Do you believe high-income taxes should fund education? Do you trust the state to spend the revenue effectively? Should a "temporary" tax be made permanent?
