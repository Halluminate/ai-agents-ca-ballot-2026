I can't tell you how to vote — that's your call based on your values. But here's the quickest way to decide:

- **Yes** if you think state and local governments should be *allowed* to create public campaign financing programs (supporters argue this reduces big-money influence in politics).
- **No** if you want to keep the ban on taxpayer money funding campaigns (opponents argue taxpayers shouldn't pay for politicians' campaigns).

One key point: the prop doesn't create a public funding program — it just removes the prohibition, letting governments adopt one if they choose.
