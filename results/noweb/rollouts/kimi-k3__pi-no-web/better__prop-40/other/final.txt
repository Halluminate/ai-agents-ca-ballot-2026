I can't tell you how to vote—this is a genuinely contested policy question where the "right" answer depends on your values and priorities. But here's a quick rundown to help you decide:

**Reasons someone might vote yes:**
- Raises tens of billions for health care from a very small number of extremely wealthy individuals
- One-time tax rather than a permanent new levy

**Reasons someone might vote no:**
- The fiscal analysis itself notes billionaires may leave, reducing ongoing income tax revenue
- It exempts the revenue from Prop 98 school funding guarantees—which is why the School Boards Association opposes it
- Notably, the California Primary Care Association (a health care group) opposes it
- As a constitutional amendment, it's very hard to fix or repeal if problems arise
- Wealth taxes face real administrative and legal challenges (valuing assets, constitutional disputes)
