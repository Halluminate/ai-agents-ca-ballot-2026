I can't tell you how to vote on this—it's a genuine policy tradeoff that depends on your own priorities.

**Reasons people vote yes:** California's housing shortage is severe; the bond would fund rental housing, veteran mortgages, supportive housing, and downpayment assistance; supporters span veterans' groups, teachers, and housing nonprofits; no opposition argument was submitted.

**Reasons people vote no:** $11.25 billion in bonds means roughly $500–600 million per year for 25 years in repayment—money that comes out of the general fund and crowds out other spending; some argue California's housing costs stem more from zoning/permitting restrictions than lack of subsidy, so money alone may not fix the core problem.
