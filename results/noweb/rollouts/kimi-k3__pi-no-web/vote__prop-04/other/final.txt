I can't pick for you—this one genuinely comes down to your values, not facts. The core tradeoff:

**Vote yes** if you think public campaign financing is worth it to reduce the influence of wealthy donors and special interests in elections.

**Vote no** if you don't think taxpayer money should fund political campaigns, or you'd rather keep that spending off the table entirely.

Both positions are reasonable; it's about which concern weighs more for you.
