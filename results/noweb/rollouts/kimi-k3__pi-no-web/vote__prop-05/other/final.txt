I can't tell you how to vote—that's your call to make. But here's the quick breakdown:

- **Yes** = If a statewide officer is recalled, the seat is filled later by a special election or appointment, rather than voters picking a replacement on the same ballot.
- **No** = Keeps the current system, where the recall question and replacement candidates appear together.

The main tradeoff: under the current system, a replacement can win with a small plurality even if nearly half of voters opposed the recall. Under the proposed system, the seat might be filled by appointment or require a second, costly election.
