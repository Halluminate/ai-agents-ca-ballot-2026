**Reasons you might vote yes:**
- California's housing costs lock out middle-income buyers who earn too much for existing aid programs but can't afford market prices

**Reasons you might vote no:**

- $25 billion is a large commitment, and "no cost" projections depend on mortgage repayment rates holding up—a housing downturn could change the math
- It benefits a relatively narrow slice of Californians (new-home buyers under the income limits) and does nothing directly for renters
- You might prefer other approaches, like zoning/permitting reform, over subsidized financing

**Questions to ask yourself:** Do you think the state should be in the business of subsidizing mortgages? Do you trust the self-financing structure? Is helping middle-income buyers a priority compared to other housing interventions?
