**Points in favor:**
- Official analysis says no direct cost to the state or taxpayers—mortgage repayments service the bonds
- No organized opposition submitted an argument

**Points worth weighing:**

- It only applies to *newly constructed* homes, so it partly functions as a subsidy to homebuilders and may push up new-home prices by adding demand
- A 17% assist may not move the needle much on affordability in high-cost markets, while being generous in cheaper ones
