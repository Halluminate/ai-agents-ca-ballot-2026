The most likely outcome is that Prop 40 fails. As of Nov. 2, 2026, the day before the election, no results had been reported.

- **Polls:** The final two major independent polls both had it losing: PPIC at 46% yes to 52% no, and Berkeley IGS at 42% to 50%. In the last round, polls showing it ahead had it only narrowly in front and below 50%: SurveyUSA at 44% to 43%, and Citrin-POLITICO at 46% to 43%. SurveyUSA also found people who had already voted chose no, 65% to 30%. California tax measures polling under 50% this late usually fail.
- **Trend:** Support has dropped steadily, from a 19-point lead in March to behind in late October.
- **Money and endorsements:** Opponents raised roughly $430 million against about $33 million for supporters. Governor Newsom, the California Teachers Association, Planned Parenthood of California and most major newspapers oppose it. In its final days, the sponsoring union's own field staff went on strike, halting their campaign work.
- **Betting markets:** Polymarket and Kalshi put its chance of passing at about 23–26%.

Higher turnout among Latino and younger voters could help the yes side, but it would still need a bigger polling miss than is typical.
