My research says Prop 39, the voter ID amendment, will probably fail. The election is November 3, 2026, and no results were out when I checked.

- **Independent polls:** Most show it losing by a lot. The late-October Berkeley IGS poll had it at 37% yes, 57% no, and PPIC had 41% yes, 58% no.
- **Republican polls:** Even the Republican pollsters M3 Strategies and McLaughlin show it behind. The backer, Carl DeMaio, said his own polling had it down 44% to 48%. A few polls show it ahead (SurveyUSA, Mason-Dixon and the Independent Voter Project), but none above 50%. Undecided voters usually end up voting no on ballot measures.
- **Betting markets:** Kalshi and Polymarket give it about a 13–15% chance of passing.
- **Other factors:** The official ballot title says it "prohibits citizens from voting" without ID. Newsom, Becerra, the Democratic Party and labor groups oppose it and have tied it to Trump. Democrats make up about half of the ballots returned so far.
