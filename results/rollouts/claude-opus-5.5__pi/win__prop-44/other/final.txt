I found no official results, so this is a prediction for the November 3, 2026 vote on Prop 44, which would require community health clinics to spend 90% of their revenue on program services. The "no" side is far more likely to win.

- **Polls:** PPIC found 34% yes and 61% no among likely voters in September. By October 16–22 it was 25% yes and 71% no, with at least six in ten voting no in every party, demographic group and region.
- **Money:** Opponents raised about $38.6 million and the backers about $16.9 million, most of it spent getting the measure on the ballot. The sponsoring union, SEIU-UHW, spent only about $20,000 on the campaign between July 1 and September 19.
- **Opposition:** Against it are both state parties, the governor, the California Medical Association, the teachers' union, Planned Parenthood and more than 300 groups and 1,000 clinics. The union is the measure's only major backer.
- **History:** Voters rejected all three of the same union's dialysis-clinic measures (2018, 2020 and 2022) by wide margins.
- **Forecast:** On Point Politics' model gives it about a 0.1% chance of passing.
