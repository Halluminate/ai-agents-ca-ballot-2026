**No.** Vote No on Prop 45.

*Optional context, since this is a close call that depends on your values:* The measure pairs sensible, enforceable deadlines with several sweeping changes.
- It sharply limits court review and bars courts from rescinding approvals.
- It lets developers choose the single alternative studied.
- Its "essential project" definition is very broad, covering freeways, edge-of-town subdivisions and dams.
- It takes a two-thirds legislative vote to amend, and amendments must further the measure's purposes.

Most urban infill housing was already exempted from environmental review in 2025, so the Legislature could pass the deadlines in a narrower bill.

The best case for Yes is faster, more predictable approvals for housing, power lines, hospitals and water. If that is your top priority and you're comfortable with less court oversight, Yes is defensible.
