**Proposition 2: Increases State's Rainy Day Fund (Legislative Constitutional Amendment)**
* **What it does:** Increases the maximum size of California's Budget Stabilization Account (Rainy Day Fund) from 10% to 20% of General Fund revenues and excludes certain reserve deposits from the state's constitutional spending limit (Gann Limit).
* **Arguments for "Yes":** Supporters (including educators, healthcare groups, and firefighters) argue it strengthens fiscal stability, prevents overspending during economic booms, and protects funding for schools, healthcare, and public safety during recessions.
* **Arguments for "No":** Opponents argue that exempting reserve deposits from the state spending limit weakens constitutional caps on government spending and reduces the likelihood of surplus tax rebates being returned to taxpayers.
