I cannot tell you whether to vote "yes" or "no" on Proposition 37, as voting decisions depend on your personal values and priorities. Here is a summary of the measure to help you decide:

* **What it does:** Authorizes $25 billion in revenue bonds to offer fixed-rate second mortgages (covering up to 17% of the purchase price) for middle-income Californians buying newly constructed homes under roughly $1.5 million, allowing buyers to put down as little as 3%.
* **A YES vote means:** You support creating this state-administered homebuying assistance program funded by private revenue bonds and repaid through homeowners' mortgage payments, which supporters argue helps middle-class buyers afford new homes at no direct cost to taxpayers.
* **A NO vote means:** You oppose creating this program, aligning with critics who argue the state should not intervene in the mortgage market or encourage lower down payments and higher household debt rather than addressing underlying housing supply costs.
