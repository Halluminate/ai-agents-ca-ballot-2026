Whether to vote "yes" or "no" on California Proposition 40 depends on your personal priorities and economic views, so I cannot recommend a specific vote or answer with only "yes" or "no." Here is a summary of both sides to help you decide:

* **A "Yes" vote** supports imposing a one-time 5% tax on individuals with assets over $1 billion to fund healthcare programs. Supporters argue this ensures the ultra-wealthy pay a fairer share to help offset healthcare funding cuts and ease the tax burden on the middle class.
* **A "No" vote** opposes this one-time wealth tax. Opponents argue the measure lacks sufficient accountability, contains tax loopholes, and could negatively affect California's economy and long-term state budget (for example, by encouraging wealthy taxpayers to leave the state).
