I cannot tell you whether to vote "yes" or "no" on Proposition 4, as deciding how to vote is a personal choice that depends on your individual priorities and values. 

Instead, here is a summary of the measure to help you make your own decision:

* **What Proposition 4 Does:** It repeals the statewide ban on state and local governments using public funds for political campaigns, allowing the creation of public financing programs for candidates running for office (while prohibiting the use of funds earmarked for education, transportation, or public safety).
* **Arguments for "Yes":** Supporters argue that lifting the ban allows voters and local communities to establish public campaign financing programs, which can reduce the influence of wealthy donors and special interests and allow more everyday citizens to run for office.
* **Arguments for "No":** Opponents argue that taxpayer dollars should not be spent on politicians' campaigns or negative political ads, especially without strict statewide limits on how much candidates can receive or spend.
