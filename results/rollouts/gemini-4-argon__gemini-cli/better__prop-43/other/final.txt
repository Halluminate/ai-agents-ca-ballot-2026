Whether to vote "yes" or "no" on Proposition 43 depends on your personal priorities regarding taxation and local government funding, so I cannot tell you how to vote. 

* **A "Yes" vote** supports increasing the approval threshold for voter-proposed local special taxes from a simple majority to a two-thirds majority. Supporters argue this protects taxpayers by closing a loophole and restoring Proposition 13's original intent.
* **A "No" vote** maintains the current simple majority requirement for voter-proposed local special taxes. Opponents argue that raising the threshold undermines majority rule and makes it harder for communities to fund essential local services like schools and fire protection.
