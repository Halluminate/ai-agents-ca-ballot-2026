Whether to vote "yes" or "no" on Proposition 5 depends on your priorities regarding California's recall election process, so I cannot recommend a single vote:

* **A "Yes" vote** removes the replacement candidate question from recall ballots for statewide officers. Supporters argue this prevents a replacement candidate from winning office with a small plurality of the vote and keeps the focus strictly on the performance of the incumbent, filling any resulting vacancy through the regular succession/appointment process or a separate special election.
* **A "No" vote** keeps the current two-part recall ballot where voters simultaneously decide whether to recall an official and who should replace them. Opponents argue that removing the replacement question takes away voters' right to immediately choose a successor, gives political insiders power through appointments, and could require costly extra special elections.
