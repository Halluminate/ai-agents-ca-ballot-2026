I cannot tell you whether to vote "yes" or "no" on a ballot measure, as the choice depends on your personal priorities and values. Here is a summary of **California Proposition 39** (Voter Identification and Citizenship Verification) to help you decide:

* **Vote YES** if you want to amend the California Constitution to require voters to present government-issued ID when voting in person, require the last four digits of a government ID number on mail-in ballot envelopes, require the state to provide free voter ID cards upon request, and mandate state and county verification of voter citizenship.
  * *Supporters argue* this increases public trust and election integrity, aligns California with voter ID standards in other states, and reduces reliance on signature verification.
* **Vote NO** if you want to keep the current law, where a voter’s identity is verified primarily through their signature at registration and on ballot envelopes without requiring government ID each time they vote.
  * *Opponents argue* this measure creates unnecessary barriers for eligible voters, exposes sensitive ID numbers on mail-in envelopes to potential identity theft, and would cost state and local governments tens of millions to low hundreds of millions of dollars annually.
