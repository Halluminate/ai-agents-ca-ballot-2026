I cannot tell you whether to vote "yes" or "no" on Proposition 43, as that decision depends on your personal priorities regarding taxation and local government funding. Instead, I can summarize what each choice means so you can decide for yourself:

* **Vote YES** if you believe voter-proposed local special taxes should require a **two-thirds supermajority (66.67%)** to pass. Supporters (such as taxpayer and business associations) argue this protects taxpayers and closes a loophole to restore the original intent of Proposition 13.
* **Vote NO** if you believe voter-proposed local special taxes should continue to pass with a **simple majority (over 50%)**. Opponents (such as firefighter, teacher, and healthcare unions) argue a higher threshold makes it too difficult for communities to fund essential local services like fire protection, schools, and hospitals.
