Whether to vote "yes" or "no" on California Proposition 2 (Increases State's Rainy Day Fund) is a personal decision that depends on your priorities for the state budget, so I cannot recommend a single vote. 

Here is an overview of the measure to help you decide:

* **A "Yes" vote** supports increasing the maximum size of the state's Rainy Day Fund from 10% to 20% of the state's budget and extending requirements for extra debt payments. Supporters argue this builds stronger reserves during economic booms to protect funding for essential services like education and healthcare during downturns.
* **A "No" vote** opposes expanding the Rainy Day Fund limit, keeping the current 10% cap in place. Opponents argue that raising the cap creates a loophole around state spending limits, which could reduce potential taxpayer rebates without guaranteeing new funding for public services.
