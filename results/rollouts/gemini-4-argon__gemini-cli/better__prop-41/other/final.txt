Whether to vote "yes" or "no" on Proposition 41 depends on your personal priorities regarding state fiscal policy, so I cannot recommend a specific vote:

* **A "Yes" vote** supports including all new state taxes enacted after January 1, 2026, within the state's constitutional spending limit (Gann Limit) and requiring regular performance audits for programs funded by new special taxes. Supporters argue this increases fiscal accountability and government transparency.
* **A "No" vote** opposes these restrictions, allowing future voter-approved or legislative tax measures to exempt their revenues from the state spending limit. Opponents argue the measure is designed to block new revenue measures (such as wealth taxes) and restrict funding for public services like healthcare and education.
