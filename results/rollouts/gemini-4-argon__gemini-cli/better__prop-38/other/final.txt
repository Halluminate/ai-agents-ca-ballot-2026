Whether to vote "yes" or "no" on Proposition 38 is a matter of personal judgment and depends on how you weigh its potential medical benefits against its fiscal impact, so I cannot tell you how to vote. 

* **A "Yes" vote** favors authorizing $8.4 billion in state bonds to fund immunology and immunotherapy research (including cancer and Alzheimer's treatments) and prescription drug discounts, prioritizing medical innovation and potential long-term healthcare breakthroughs.
* **A "No" vote** opposes issuing these bonds, prioritizing fiscal restraint by avoiding long-term state debt and keeping funding decisions within the standard legislative budget process to preserve flexibility for other state priorities.
