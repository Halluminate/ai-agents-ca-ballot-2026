* **A "Yes" vote** supports prohibiting the state from enacting new taxes on personal property ownership (such as wealth taxes on stocks, retirement accounts, or savings) and barring certain retroactive taxes. **Supporters** argue this protects residents' savings and retirement accounts from double taxation, provides tax predictability, and keeps businesses and investments in California.
