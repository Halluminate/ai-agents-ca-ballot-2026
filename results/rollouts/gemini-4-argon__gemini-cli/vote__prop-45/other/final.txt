I cannot tell you whether to vote "yes" or "no" on a ballot proposition, as voting decisions depend on your personal priorities and values. Instead, here is a summary of what **California Proposition 45** does and the main arguments on both sides to help you decide:

**Overview:**
Proposition 45 modifies the California Environmental Quality Act (CEQA) to expedite environmental reviews and legal challenges for specified housing, transportation, water, and health projects.

* **A "Yes" vote** supports streamlining CEQA environmental reviews and legal timelines for these projects. **Supporters argue** this will cut bureaucratic delays and legal roadblocks, helping build essential housing and infrastructure faster and lowering the cost of living.
* **A "No" vote** opposes changing current CEQA review processes for these projects. **Opponents argue** the measure weakens vital environmental and public health safeguards, reduces community oversight, and could negatively affect air quality and local ecosystems.
