Whether to vote "yes" or "no" on Proposition 3 is a value judgment that depends on your personal priorities, so there is no single objectively better choice:

- **A "Yes" vote** makes the existing higher personal income tax rates on individuals earning over $371,000 permanent in order to continue funding public education and healthcare programs. Supporters favor this to maintain revenue for public services without raising taxes on the majority of Californians.
- **A "No" vote** allows those temporary tax increases to expire as scheduled. Opponents favor this to avoid making temporary taxes permanent, citing California's high overall tax burden and advocating for stricter government spending discipline instead.
