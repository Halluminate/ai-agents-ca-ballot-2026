Whether to vote "yes" or "no" on Proposition 44 is a personal decision that depends on your priorities, so I cannot recommend a specific vote or answer with only "yes" or "no." Here is a neutral overview to help you decide:

* **Vote YES** if you believe private nonprofit Federally Qualified Health Centers should be legally required to spend at least 90% of their annual revenue on direct program services (such as patient care), and you want to increase financial accountability and limit administrative or executive overhead. (Supported by SEIU-UHW).
* **Vote NO** if you are concerned that a strict 90% spending mandate and associated penalties are too inflexible for community clinics, could strain their operations, and risk reducing services or closing clinics in underserved communities. (Opposed by the California Medical Association, American Academy of Pediatrics CA, and California Academy of Family Physicians).
