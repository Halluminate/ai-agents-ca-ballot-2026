Whether to vote "yes" or "no" on Proposition 39 is a personal decision that depends on your priorities, so I cannot recommend a specific vote or answer with only "yes" or "no." 

Here is an overview of both sides to help you decide:

* **A "Yes" vote** supports amending the California Constitution to require voters to present government-issued identification when voting in person, or to provide the last four digits of a government ID on mail-in ballot envelopes (while requiring the state to provide free voter ID cards upon request). Supporters argue this is a common-sense measure that strengthens election security and boosts public confidence in election results.
* **A "No" vote** opposes adding these new voter ID requirements and maintains the current system, where most voters do not need to show ID after their initial voter registration is verified. Opponents argue that the measure creates unnecessary barriers for eligible voters—particularly low-income, elderly, and marginalized residents—and costs taxpayers money to solve a problem of voter impersonation that is already exceedingly rare.
