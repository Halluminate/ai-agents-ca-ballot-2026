**Polling trajectory (PPIC, the main pollster):**

- One smaller poll (Probolsky) showed Yes narrowly ahead, but it's a lone outlier with unpublished toplines

The weight of the evidence — the most recent major polling, the consistent downward trend, the forecast model, and coalition structure — favors the No side.
