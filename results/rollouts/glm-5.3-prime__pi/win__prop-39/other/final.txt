Based on my extensive research on California Proposition 39 (the November 2026 voter ID initiative):

**Key evidence:**
- **Most recent polling consistently shows the measure losing**: Berkeley IGS (Oct 19–25) has No leading 57%–37%; PPIC (Oct 16–22) has No up 58%–41%; Citrin/Politico has No up 51%–42%. Even Republican-leaning pollsters (M3 Strategies, McLaughlin) show it trailing slightly.
- **Clear downward trend**: The measure led 56%–39% in April, but support steadily eroded as the opposition campaign ramped up — a classic pattern for California ballot initiatives.
- **Hostile political environment**: California's electorate is heavily Democratic (80% of Democrats/Dem-leaners oppose), and opponents successfully tied the measure to Trump, whose administration is actively litigating against California's election practices. The unified opposition includes Gov. Newsom, gubernatorial frontrunner Xavier Becerra (leading his race by ~16 points), the state Democratic Party, labor unions, ACLU, Common Cause, and the League of Women Voters.
- **Fundraising is roughly at parity** (Yes: ~$36.5M, mostly from out-of-state billionaires; No: ~$33.5M from the Democratic Party, unions, and tribes), so the Yes side's early money advantage no longer insulates it.
