Based on my extensive research, here is what I found regarding California Proposition 44 (November 3, 2026 ballot):

**Key evidence:**

1. **Polling (most decisive factor):** The final PPIC Statewide Survey (Oct 16–22, 2026) shows just **25% yes vs. 71% no** among likely voters, with support having *dropped* from 34% in September. Six in ten or more voters across every partisan, demographic, and regional group oppose it. It has consistently been the least popular proposition on the ballot.

2. **Campaign spending:** The opposition (California Primary Care Association Advocates) has raised **$38.6 million** vs. **$16.9 million** for supporters. SEIU-UHW (the sole sponsor) spent only ~$20,000 on the actual campaign from July 1–Sept 19 while opponents spent $32+ million — the sponsor has essentially abandoned advertising for it, while opponents launched a massive $44 million counteroffensive.

3. **Endorsements:** Opponents include the California Democratic, Republican, and Working Families parties, Governor Newsom, Planned Parenthood, the California Medical Association, CA Hospital Association, American Academy of Pediatrics-CA, and essentially every major newspaper editorial board (SF Chronicle, LA Daily News, Sac Bee/McClatchy, Mercury News, San Diego UT, Press Democrat).

4. **Track record:** SEIU-UHW's three similar healthcare clinic measures (dialysis initiatives in 2018, 2020, 2022) all failed at the polls.

The "No" side is heavily favored to win.
