Research summary supporting this conclusion:

**Proposition 40** (Nov. 3, 2026 California ballot) would impose a one-time 5% wealth tax on billionaires to fund health care. The "No" side is more likely to win, based on:

1. **Latest polling shows the measure trailing**: The two most reputable California pollsters both show it losing in late October — PPIC found 46% Yes / 52% No (down from 52% Yes a month earlier), and UC Berkeley IGS found 42% Yes / 50% No. Even the most favorable poll (Citrin-POLITICO) had it at only 46% support — below the majority needed to pass.

2. **The trend is clearly negative**: Support has steadily eroded from a 19-point lead in March (52%-33%) to underwater in the final week, with undecideds breaking toward No.

3. **Massive spending disparity**: Billionaire opponents (led by Sergey Brin's $102M, plus Eric Schmidt, John Doerr, and Patrick Collison, via "Building a Better California") have outspent supporters roughly 9-to-1 — over $300 million vs. ~$33 million, nearly all from a single union (SEIU-UHW). This is a record-breaking opposition effort.

4. **Unusual breadth of opposition**: The No coalition spans the political spectrum — California Teachers Association, California Medical Association, firefighters, building trades, school boards, taxpayer groups, Gov. Gavin Newsom, and both Democrats and Republicans.

5. **Prediction markets agree**: Both Kalshi and Polymarket put Prop 40's odds of passage below 30%.
