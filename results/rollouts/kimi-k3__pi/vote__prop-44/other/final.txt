**No.**

Based on extensive research — the LAO analysis, the Hoover Institution's breakdown, news investigations, campaign finance records, and editorial boards across the state — Prop 44 is a badly designed measure with a troubling provenance:

- Clinics currently average ~80% spending on services, so the vast majority would instantly face massive penalties (potentially $1.6B+ in year one) — while federal Medicaid cuts are already straining them.
- The 90% floor is measured against total *revenue*, penalizing clinics for prudent reserves/surpluses, and key patient services (interpretation, transportation, case management) may not count.
- It's funded solely by SEIU-UHW, which ran three failed dialysis measures as leverage tactics; the LA Times reported the union offered to drop this measure in exchange for help unionizing clinic workers, prompting a federal racketeering lawsuit. The failed legislative version even exempted clinics that unionized.
- Opposed by the CA Medical Association, pediatricians, family physicians, school nurses, Planned Parenthood, both major parties, and every editorial board I found; polling shows voters rejecting it ~2-to-1.

**Vote No.**
