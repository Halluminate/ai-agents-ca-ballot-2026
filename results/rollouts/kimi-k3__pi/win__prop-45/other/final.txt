**Research summary — California Proposition 45 (Nov 3, 2026): "Modifies Environmental Review for Certain Projects" (CEQA reform initiative)**

Evidence gathered:
- **PPIC poll (Oct 16–22, the gold-standard nonpartisan survey, 1,086 likely voters):** 46% yes / 50% no / 4% undecided — the first time the measure has trailed, down from 49–46 ahead in September and 73% in July (with generic language). Clear downward trend.
- **Probolsky Research poll (Oct 27):** Yes narrowly ahead, but full toplines not published.
- **OnPoint Politics forecast (Nov 2):** No slightly favored — Yes has a 47.8% chance to pass, No 52.3%.
- **Manifold prediction market:** ~30% chance of passing.
- **Structural factors:** Spending is roughly even (~$40M each side); the Democratic Party and environmental groups oppose it; Gov. Newsom stayed neutral. Critically, California initiatives polling below 50% in late October rarely pass — undecided voters historically break heavily toward "no" (status quo bias), and the yes side sits at only 46% in the best poll with a deteriorating trend.

The preponderance of evidence favors the "No" side.
