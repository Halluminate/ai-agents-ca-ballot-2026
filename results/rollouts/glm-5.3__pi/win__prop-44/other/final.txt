- The No side (California Primary Care Association) raised ~$38.6 million and ran a massive ad campaign through Election Day

**Endorsements:**
- Prop 44 is the only measure opposed by *both* major parties (Democratic and Republican), plus the Working Families Party, Planned Parenthood, the California Medical Association, Gov.
