I've completed extensive research on this proposition. Here's a summary of what I found before my decision:

**What Prop 41 (Nov. 2026) does:**
- Requires State Auditor audits of programs funded by new special taxes (before ballot qualification and every 4 years after), with a mandated 10% cost-cutting recommendation
- Prohibits/nullifies state taxes enacted after Jan 1, 2026 that exempt revenues from the state spending limit (Gann Limit)

**Key findings from my research:**
- Neutral observers (Legislative Analyst's Office, CalMatters, Berkeley IGS, CA Budget & Policy Center, KQED) widely identify its **primary purpose as nullifying Prop 40** (the billionaire wealth tax) if it receives more votes — a "poison pill"
- It's funded overwhelmingly by billionaires with a direct financial stake: Sergey Brin (~$57M+), Patrick Collison, Eric Schmidt, John Doerr, Stewart Resnick, Tony Xu
- The audit provisions are largely redundant (the State Auditor and LAO already perform these functions), while the mandated 10% cut findings could pressure real service cuts even for efficient programs
- The Gann Limit provision ties new taxes to a 1979-based formula, and analysts warn it could jeopardize Medi-Cal financing mechanisms (MCO tax, Hospital QA fee) that draw billions in federal matching funds
- Even editorial boards that **oppose** Prop 40 itself (LA Times, Mercury News/East Bay Times) recommend No on 41, calling it deceptive games-playing; the SF Chronicle called it "a cynical poison pill"
- Opposed by the CA Democratic Party, League of Women Voters, CA State PTA, ACLU of SoCal, and Bernie Sanders; supported mainly by business/taxpayer groups and the CA Republican Party
