Deciding whether to vote yes or no depends on your policy priorities, so I cannot recommend one over the other:

- **Yes:** Raises the requirement to approve voter-proposed local special taxes to a two-thirds supermajority, prioritizing taxpayer protections against local tax increases.
- **No:** Keeps the requirement at a simple majority (over 50%), prioritizing the ability of local communities to fund public services like emergency response, schools, and infrastructure.
