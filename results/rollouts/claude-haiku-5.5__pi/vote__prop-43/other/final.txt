**No.** It would make citizen-initiated special taxes for things like fire, schools, and housing much harder to pass. In 1998–2012, city and county special taxes averaged about 63% yes, below the two-thirds bar, and recent measures like LA's Measure ULA (58%) and Sonoma's fire tax (62%) would have failed. Its revenue effect is uncertain, and as a constitutional amendment it would be hard to undo. If you think any earmarked tax should need two-thirds regardless of who proposes it, vote Yes.
