**No** (a no vote keeps current rules).

Most clinics it covers already fall short of its 90% test, so the penalty would initially hit most of them. The Attorney General would define what counts as qualifying spending only after the vote. Physician groups, both major parties, Gov. Newsom, and every major editorial board I found oppose it. The sponsor, SEIU-UHW, has a long record of using ballot measures as leverage in labor fights, and it faces a federal lawsuit alleging extortion, which it denies. Concern about executive pay is legitimate, but this is a blunt tool, and voters who weigh that concern more heavily could reasonably vote yes.
